About KDS Dairy Products

Pure | Honest | Traditional

Our Story

KDS Dairy Products was started with a simple goal — to deliver 100% pure, traditional desi ghee to every household. Our journey began from a small family farm in West Bengal, where we followed ancient bilona methods to preserve taste, nutrition and purity.

Today, we proudly serve families across West Bengal with premium quality ghee prepared using time-tested traditional techniques.

Founder – Maloy Guha

Maloy Guha is the founder and driving force behind KDS Dairy Products. With deep respect for traditional dairy methods and commitment to quality, he ensures that every batch of ghee meets the highest purity standards.
