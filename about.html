About KDS Dairy Products

Pure | Honest | Traditional

Our Story

KDS Dairy Products was started with a simple goal — to deliver 100% pure, traditional desi ghee to every household. Our journey began from a small family farm in West Bengal, where we followed traditional methods to preserve taste, nutrition and purity.

Today, we proudly serve families across West Bengal and all over India, and export our premium quality ghee to multiple countries worldwide, delivering purity, authenticity, and traditional excellence in every drop.

Mr. Debargha Guha

Founder

Mr. Debargha Guha is the visionary founder of KDS Dairy Products. His passion for pure dairy and traditional practices laid the foundation of the brand with a commitment to authenticity and excellence.

Mr. Maloy Guha

CEO

Maloy Guha is the driving force behind daily operations and quality control at KDS Dairy Products. With 15+ years of experience, he ensures every batch meets the highest purity and hygiene standards.
